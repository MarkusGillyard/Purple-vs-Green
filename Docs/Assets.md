## Reproducing the casting samples

The KayKit archive SHA-256 is `65882f31f905ad2e953819648a59287cdeab8f623908d5ef701971d3758be20f`.
Extract `Rig_Medium_CombatRanged.fbx` from the FBX files. Its SHA-256 is
`b70e1d5ef64fafaad5c50ca2225c238f33ec9622876014f38478deeb10ee2ba4`.
The [art instructions](../Tools/Art/README.md) describe regeneration. The checked-in
samples are sufficient for builds from a clean checkout.

The evaluated Fab listings require acquisition under their displayed license and
access through Epic's supported tools for Unreal-format content. Their payloads
are outside this project. The public repository distributes project-owned armor,
the derived CC0 samples, and source references to engine content.
